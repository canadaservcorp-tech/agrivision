Platform Capabilities

Everything your farm needs in one system

From field sensors to AI diagnostics, Agrivision covers the full crop monitoring lifecycle.

🌱

AI Disease Detection

Camera modules powered by TensorFlow Lite scan crops for 348+ disease patterns. Identifies issues before they spread.

📡

IoT Sensor Network

Raspberry Pi CM4 hardware with DHT22, soil moisture, and ADS1115 sensors. Real-time environmental data from every zone.

🛰️

Satellite & Weather

Integrated NDVI satellite imagery and local weather forecasts. Plan irrigation, spraying, and harvesting with precision.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Visual charts showing yield trends, sensor history, and crop health scores. Export reports for stakeholders.

🔔

Smart Alerts

Push notifications when thresholds are breached — drought stress, pest detection, temperature spikes, equipment issues.

📱

Mobile App (iOS & Android)

Full control from your phone. Live sensor data, manual capture trigger, multi-device management across fields.

🔄

OTA Updates

Remote firmware updates push new AI models to field devices without physical access. Zero downtime.

🌐

4G LTE Connectivity

Quectel EC25 modules ensure reliable upload from remote locations. Offline caching, auto-sync when restored.

🗂️

Multi-Farm Management

Manage multiple sites from one account. Assign teams, set per-zone alerts, compare performance across regions.

💊

Treatment Recommendations

AI-generated treatment plans based on disease type, severity, and crop stage. Organic and IPM options with dosage calculations.

💧

Smart Irrigation

AI combines soil moisture, weather forecast, and crop type to calculate exact irrigation timing. Never over or under-water again.

🍎

Production Monitoring

Track fruit diameter, length, and weight. Daily production reports with available quantities — harvest at peak quality.

📄

Export & Insurance Reports

Generate PDF/CSV reports for stakeholders and insurance claims. Timestamped detection logs as verifiable evidence. ROI tracking.

☀️

Solar Power Ready

Hardware supports 12V to 5V buck converter with XT60 connectors. Designed for solar panel and battery field deployment.

💾

Offline Data Caching

Local SQLite database stores sensor readings and captured images when connectivity drops. Auto-uploads when 4G restores.

🔋

Device Health Monitoring

Tracks battery level, device temperature, 4G signal strength, and storage usage. Alerts when maintenance is needed.

🔒

Farmer Privacy Controls

Opt-in by default for company contact. Farmers can disable all company contact, block companies, and control data visibility.

🌍

Global Farmer Community

Connect with farmers worldwide. Share experiences, solutions, and real-world treatment results. Research API for institutions.

How It Works

From field to phone in 3 steps

Set up once, monitor forever. No IT team required.

01

Install the module

Mount the Agrivision unit in your field. Flash SD card, connect sensors, power on. Under 30 minutes.

02

Connect & configure

Auto-connects via 4G. Download mobile app, scan QR code, set crop type and alert thresholds.

03

Monitor & act

Real-time data, disease alerts, recommendations. Dashboards, reports, data-driven decisions daily.
