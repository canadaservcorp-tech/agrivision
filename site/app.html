Farmer Mobile App

Your entire farm in your pocket. Monitor crops, receive instant disease alerts, view live camera feeds, and manage your Agrivision subscription — all from your iOS or Android device.

Key Features

📡

Live Sensor Dashboard

Real-time soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and light readings from every zone. Historical charts and trend analysis.

📷

Live Camera Feed

View your field cameras in real-time. Trigger manual captures, review past images, and zoom into detected issues.

🔔

Push Notifications

Instant alerts for disease detections, sensor threshold breaches, weather warnings, and device health issues.

🗂️

Multi-Farm Management

Switch between farms, assign zones, manage team members, and compare performance across locations.

💾

Offline Mode

Access cached data and recent reports without internet. Auto-syncs when connectivity is restored.

💳

Subscription Management

View, upgrade, or manage your subscription plan directly from the app. Integrated with PayPal billing.
