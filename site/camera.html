Camera SD Card Installer

Transform any compatible field camera into an AI-powered crop monitoring device. Simply flash the SD card, insert it, and your camera becomes part of the Agrivision ecosystem.

What It Does

The Camera SD Card Installer writes Agrivision's proprietary firmware onto a standard micro-SD card. Once inserted into a supported camera module, the firmware enables on-device AI disease detection, automatic image capture scheduling, and cloud synchronization via 4G LTE.

How It Works

1

Download & Flash

Download the Agrivision SD Card Image from your dashboard. Flash it to a micro-SD card using the installer tool.

2

Insert & Power On

Insert the SD card into your camera module. Power on the device — the firmware boots automatically.

3

Pair via QR Code

Scan the QR code shown on the camera LED screen (or printed label) with the Agrivision mobile app to link the device to your account.

4

Monitor & Detect

The camera captures images on a schedule, runs AI analysis locally, and syncs results to your dashboard and disease library.
